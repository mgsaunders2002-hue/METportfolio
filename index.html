ETEC 522 · Innovative Venture Pitch

RoboShift

A readiness-and-planning tool that helps schools grow robotics programs that last — not just robotics programs that launch.

An original venture concept addressing the implementation gap in K–12 educational robotics: schools routinely invest in kits and competition success while depending on a single teacher, reaching a narrow group of students, or lacking any real plan for curriculum, funding, or continuity. RoboShift is a diagnostic audit and planning tool that assesses a program across seven readiness dimensions, surfaces hidden risks, and translates the results into a concrete 90-day action plan. The pitch includes a business model, pilot plan, and founder-market fit case.

ETEC 522 · Analytical Report

Beyond the Robot Kit

Is educational robotics solving the right problem? An EVA analysis of the missing implementation layer.

An independent analyst report evaluating the K–12 educational robotics market for investors, funders, and education leaders. Using an Educational Venture Analyst framework, the report rates the market across demand, educational value, hardware saturation, implementation risk, and equity risk — and argues that robotics doesn't have an engagement problem, it has an implementation problem. The recommendation: invest selectively in the support layer — teacher capacity, curriculum pathways, and long-term planning — rather than another generic robot kit.

ETEC 522 · Innovative Neuroscience OER · team project

Designing for Every Brain

What would AI learning technology look like if it were designed for cognitive diversity rather than the “average” learner?

A co-authored open educational resource examining neuroscience-informed AI tools for neurodivergent learners. The module walks through executive-function research, a learner persona, and a comparison of cognitive profiles, then evaluates emerging AI ventures against a central question: does the strongest tool speed up task completion, or build understanding, memory, transfer, and independence? Closes with a market opportunity forecast identifying which category of venture merits investment. A collaborative team project, published under CC BY-SA 4.0.

ETEC 510 · Design Case Study

Polygraph — Critical AI Literacy for Educators

A professional-learning module that helps K–12 educators interrogate AI outputs before they reach the classroom.

A recursive, four-phase professional learning experience — Experiencing, Conceptualizing, Analyzing, Applying — grounded in the New London Group's multiliteracies framework. Educators build a shared vocabulary for critiquing AI outputs (training-data gaps, homogenized narratives, algorithmic bias), practice a repeatable audit routine, and leave with a personal positioning statement on AI use in their own professional context.

ETEC 511 · Case Study

A Tipping Point, Not a Reset

A comparative case study of two schools — Grays, England and Surrey, BC — and why platform-based learning outlasted the pandemic.

A comparative case study analyzing how COVID-19 pushed two schools with very different starting conditions — device access, existing platform familiarity, and family support at home — onto emergency online learning, and why neither school fully reverted afterward. Frames the pandemic as a genuine tipping point: Microsoft Teams remained embedded in school life in Grays, and Google Classroom became even more established in Surrey.

ETEC 512 · Learning Theory Module

Constructing Knowledge

An interactive site that lets visitors experience constructivist principles firsthand, not just read about them.

A multi-page interactive learning experience translating seven core constructivist principles — prior knowledge, situated learning, multiple perspectives, active learning, reflection, and social interaction — into hands-on activities across subject areas. Visitors build their own understanding as they move through the site, in keeping with the theory it teaches.
